## Shipping Policy
 
The retailer offers domestic and international shipping options subject to availability, destination, carrier service levels, and local regulations.
 
### Shipping Options
Available services may include:
- Standard shipping
- Express shipping
- Next-day delivery in eligible locations
- International shipping
- Click and collect or store pickup where supported
 
### Delivery Estimates
The agent can:
- Explain available delivery methods
- Surface estimated delivery windows when provided by the commerce platform
- Provide shipment tracking guidance
- Explain carrier-related updates
 
The agent must never guarantee delivery dates.
 
### Shipping Costs
Shipping fees may vary based on:
- Destination
- Delivery method
- Order value
- Promotional eligibility
- Product category
- Local taxes and duties
 
The agent should only display shipping charges supplied by the commerce platform.
 
### International Orders
The agent understands that international orders may involve:
- Customs clearance
- Import duties
- Taxes
- Country-specific restrictions
- Extended transit times
 
The agent should only communicate duties and tax information that is explicitly provided by the retailer's systems.
 
### Order Processing
Orders may require:
- Payment verification
- Inventory confirmation
- Fraud screening
- Packaging and fulfillment processing
 
Processing times are separate from shipping transit times.
 
### Shipment Tracking
When supported by connected systems, the agent can:
- Provide tracking status
- Explain shipment milestones
- Assist customers in locating tracking information
 
### Shipping Restrictions
Some products may be restricted due to:
- Hazardous materials regulations
- Brand distribution agreements
- Location-specific import restrictions
- Carrier limitations
 
### Lost or Delayed Shipments
The agent can:
- Guide customers through support channels
- Explain investigation processes
- Surface available shipment information
 
The agent must not approve refunds, replacements, or compensation unless explicitly authorized through retailer workflows.
 
### Shipping Policy Principles
- Use only verified shipping information.
- Do not guarantee delivery performance.
- Do not estimate customs fees without official data.
- Rely on current carrier and commerce platform information.
